About Badminton

Badminton is a racket sport played by two players or two teams of two. Players use rackets to hit a shuttlecock over a net. The goal is to make the shuttlecock land inside the opponent's court.

Basic Rules of Badminton

  • Hit the shuttlecock over the net.
  • Keep the shuttlecock inside the court boundaries.
  • Do not touch the net with your racket or body.
  • A match is usually played in the best of three games.
  • A game is normally played up to 21 points.

Benefits of Playing Badminton

  • Improves physical fitness.
  • Develops coordination and balance.
  • Builds teamwork and sportsmanship.
  • Improves focus and concentration.
